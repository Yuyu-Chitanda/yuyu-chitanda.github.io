Privacy Policy

Yuyu Chitanda, an individual developer (the "Developer"), sets out this Privacy Policy (the "Policy") regarding how user information is handled in the Android app "Tsuzuku – Habit Counter" (Japanese name: 三日坊主から脱出; the "App").

1. Information we handle

1-1. Information stored only on your device (not sent to the Developer)

The following information that you enter or set in the App is stored only on your device. It is not sent to the Developer or to any third party.

  • Habit data: habit names, colors and start dates
  • Record data: the dates on which you completed each habit
  • Settings: theme (light/dark), first day of the week (Sunday/Monday) and the sort order of the home screen

The App has no account registration or login, and the data above is not sent to or stored on any server outside your device.

1-2. Information sent automatically (crash reports)

If the App crashes, the following technical information is automatically sent to the Developer through Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google, so that the cause can be investigated and fixed (release builds only).

  • Error details at the time of the crash (stack trace)
  • Device information (model, OS version, available memory, etc.) and the App version
  • A random identifier that Firebase assigns to each installation of the App (it does not identify you personally)

Habit names, records and settings are not intentionally included in crash reports. The App does not send data for analytics or advertising.

When Firebase Crashlytics is used, technical information such as your IP address may be obtained through Google's systems. For how this information is handled, please refer to Google's privacy policy and the Firebase terms.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Privacy and Security in Firebase

2. Purposes of use

  • Data stored on your device: to provide the App's recording features, such as calculating streaks and showing the calendar
  • Crash reports: to investigate the causes of problems and improve the quality of the App

3. Provision to third parties

Except for Firebase Crashlytics (Google) described in 1-2, the App does not provide or share any information with third parties. The App does not show advertisements.

4. Sharing feature

The sharing feature on the habit detail screen turns your streak and similar information into text and opens Android's standard share menu. The text is handed to the app you choose. Whether to share and which app to share with is decided only by your own actions, and the App never sends information on its own. How the receiving app handles the text is governed by that app's privacy policy.

5. Device permissions

The only permission the App uses is the internet permission (INTERNET). It is used only to send the crash reports described in 1-2. The App does not request any other permission, such as camera, location or contacts.

6. Device backup

The App supports Android's automatic backup. If this feature is enabled on your device, the App's data may be saved to the backup storage of your Google account. This is a standard Android mechanism, and the Developer cannot access this data. You can turn backup on or off in your device's system settings.

7. Retention and deletion

Data stored on your device is deleted from the device when you uninstall the App. Crash reports are retained in accordance with Google's policies.

8. Age

The App is not aimed at any specific age group. If a child under 13 uses the App, we ask that a parent or guardian supervise.

9. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be changed without notice in response to changes in laws or the addition of features to the App. If there is an important change, we will make an effort to notify you, for example within the App.
