Tsuzuku – Habit Counter

An Android app that tracks how many days in a row you have kept each habit.

Preparing for release on Google Play

What it does

  • Manage several habits at once, each with its own color
  • Log today with one tap and see your current streak and your best streak
  • Look back with a calendar and a year view that shows each month's completed days
  • Sort the home screen by title, current streak, start date or best streak
  • Choose a light or dark theme, and start the week on Sunday or Monday
  • Show your streak to friends as a share card

Privacy

Your habits and records are stored only on your device. No account is needed and the app shows no ads.

Only when the app crashes, a crash report (device model, OS version, error details and similar) is sent automatically so the problem can be fixed. It does not include your habit names or records. See the Privacy Policy for details.

The app is available in English and Japanese. You can switch the language in Settings.
